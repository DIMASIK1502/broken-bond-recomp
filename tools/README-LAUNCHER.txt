Naruto: The Broken Bond (PC / ReXGlue)
======================================

Launch: Launcher.exe (or Start.bat / Запуск.bat).

Build Launcher.exe:

    build_launcher.bat

Place Launcher.exe next to broken_bond.exe, the ReXGlue DLLs, and a game\
folder with your extracted Xbox 360 dump.

Presets:
  Performance — native resolution, fewer hitches
  Medium      — native + CAS
  Quality     — internal 2K (prettier; first fight may hitch)

Custom settings live on the Graphics and Controls tabs.

Do not press F4 "Save to config" in-game.
Saves: Documents\broken_bond\
Logs:  logs\run.log

ROV is always forced on NVIDIA (otherwise the 3D world goes black).
Do not rename the game\ folder.
